Closing History · expanded day

Two layouts for the expanded closing-day summary

Same rows, same order, same figures — every row from the capture is still present, still rendered at zero, still in the sealed order (sales → expenses → reconciliation → after close → hand-over). What changes is hierarchy: the ~18 rows that all weigh the same today split into a quiet reference tier and a heavier result tier, so the numbers a person acts on are the ones that read first. 1a does it typographically on one flat surface; 1b does it with recessed zones. Each option shows an ordinary day in light and a drifted day in dark.

1a Tiered ledger

One flat surface, three weights. Reference rows drop to 12.5px muted; the two numbers the day turns on — Expected and Counted — sit together in a tinted reconciliation strip that carries the variance chip, so the read is "expected, counted, difference" in one glance instead of two rows nine lines apart. Group headings gain a hairline rule and a glyph. The hand-over keeps its bordered box and gets the only large type in the block.

1b Zoned summary

Each group becomes a recessed zone with its heading inside it, and every zone ends with the one line it resolves to — Cash sales, Cash expenses, Counted cash — set above the hairline as the zone's result. Scanning the block vertically gives four numbers before any detail is read. The hand-over rises out of the recess onto card surface as the block's conclusion; the duplicate "Counted" becomes a stated carry-forward (from counted cash) rather than a repeated row.

What is held constant in both
· Every row present, none hidden at zero (Plate No DP, Plate No Delivery, Labor (service) still render at ₱0.00) · Sealed order kept: sales → expenses → reconciliation → after close → hand-over · Labor (service) stays directly under gross parts · Variance semantics and both palettes unchanged · Mono money in the hand-over only · Expansion stays in place, no detail route · No figure or formula altered · Every line that moves cash on hand carries its sign: + adds, − deducts (opening float, cash sales, plate DP add; cash expenses, plate delivery deduct) · After close spans every frozen line — sales, labor fees, shop fees, expenses — but lists only the ones that moved after closing: at-closing figure → after-closing figure → updated figure, ending in updated cash on hand. Unchanged lines are omitted here (the sealed summary above still shows them all, including at ₱0.00) · SHOP FEES is its own section after SALES, broken down by fee type, and hands over to management with the rest of the counted cash — figures across all four frames were re-balanced so the arithmetic still closes
