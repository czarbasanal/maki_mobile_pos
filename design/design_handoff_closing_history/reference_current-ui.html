Closing History — current mobile UI

Reference capture of lib/presentation/mobile/screens/reports/daily_closing_history_screen.dart as it stands on main (2026-08-31, shipped in APK +31). This is what exists today, not a proposal. Two views: the list of closed days, and a day expanded in place. Light and dark are both shown because the app ships both. Every figure below is illustrative but internally consistent — the arithmetic relationships are real and must survive any redesign.

The drifted case. The drawer was closed at 5:05 PM and trading continued. After close reports what moved; the hand-over then supersedes its own sealed figures and ends with Updated cash on hand. 550 + 4,540 = 5,090 — the parts add to the footer, not to the “Counted” row at the top.

Do not change any figure or formula. Every amount here is computed elsewhere and frozen into an immutable closing document. A redesign may move, group, restyle or rename rows — it must not alter what a number means, drop a row, or hide a row at zero. Several rows exist because hiding them at zero was read as “this screen doesn’t show that”.
